01 — About

A multidisciplinary journey

I am an architect with a multidisciplinary background that combines architectural design, topographic engineering, spatial analysis, research, and teaching.

My academic journey began with training in topographic engineering, which gave me a strong foundation in spatial analysis and technical understanding before I specialized in architecture.

During my architectural studies at Universitas Islam Indonesia, I developed a strong interest in contextual design, environmental performance, simulation, and architectural research.

I also worked as an architecture teaching assistant, where I supported students in their research, scientific writing, and architectural simulation workshops.
